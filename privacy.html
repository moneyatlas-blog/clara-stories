Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026.

What we collect

Clara Stories is a simple informational website. We do not require accounts and do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal information directly.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses and browser types) for security and operational purposes.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use Clara Stories (for example, which stories are read most). Google Analytics sets cookies and collects anonymized usage data such as pages visited, approximate location, device type, and browser type. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to work. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all sites with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

We may display ads served by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdSense). These networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Ads Settings page.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links (for example, to books on Amazon). If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored nofollow".

Third-party links

Our articles link to external sources (such as our Facebook page). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will reflect the latest revision.
